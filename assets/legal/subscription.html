Billing

Payment is charged to your Google Play account at confirmation of purchase.

Free Trial

If a trial is offered, Google sends a reminder 2 days before it ends.

Manage

Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Cancel

Select subscription → Cancel subscription → follow instructions.

Subscriptions renew each billing cycle unless canceled.
