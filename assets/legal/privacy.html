Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we store on your device

Selected server, onboarding state, and debug logs. This data never leaves your phone.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, or browsing history.

VPN Gate relay servers

OxVPN routes traffic through public VPN Gate servers. These are operated by third parties and log connections for academic research. Do not use for sensitive activities.

Ads and analytics

If enabled, Google Mobile Ads and Firebase Analytics may collect device identifiers. You can opt out via Google settings.
